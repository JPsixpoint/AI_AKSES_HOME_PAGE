## 📇 CRM View – Relationship Intelligence  
> The CRM in AKSES is your intelligence layer for people, companies, and interactions — not just deals.

While your **Pipeline** tracks deal progress and underwriting, the **CRM View** gives you visibility into:
- All contacts across deals and companies  
- Your firm's relationship network  
- Meeting, email, and interaction history  
- Origination performance and gaps  
- AI-powered insights into who matters, who’s active, and who’s missing

---

### 🔍 Core Capabilities

#### 👥 **Contact & Company Universe**
- Unified directory of all people across all deals
- Contact fields: Name, Title, Role (e.g. CEO, Investment Manager), Email, Phone, LinkedIn
- Company fields: Sector, Region, Portfolio Status (New, Live, Exited)
- Relationship Map: See which companies a contact is tied to, and vice versa
- Tags: `Warm`, `Cold`, `IC`, `Origination Source`, `Repeat Founder`

#### 🗓️ **Interaction History**
- Meeting recordings (via Grain) with AI summaries
- Logged emails, DMs, and Slack messages (optional sync)
- Notes linked to people or companies
- Last contact date
- Interaction streaks or gaps (AI: “90 days silent”)

#### 🔁 **Origination Intelligence**
- Track deal sources: who brought in the most deals this quarter
- Identify drop-off patterns: who hasn't sent a deal in X months
- AI summary: “Top 10 high-potential but inactive contacts”
- Graph view of intro paths (e.g., LP → Analyst → Deal)

---

### 📥 Deal Import & AI Mapping
> Use AKSES CRM to ingest and normalize your deal and contact universe.

- **Import from:**
  - HubSpot
  - DealCloud
  - CSV / Excel

- **AI-Powered Mapping:**
  - Match imported deals to existing people + companies
  - Merge or dedupe records automatically
  - Tag new entries by stage, source, geography
  - Suggest missing contacts (e.g., “this deal has no founder contact attached”)

---

- “Summarize all past touchpoints with John Morales from Monet”

### 🔐 Access Control
- Role-based visibility: Analyst, Partner, Ops  
- Tags for filtering (`Investor`, `Operator`, `IC Member`)  
- Export control by permission level
